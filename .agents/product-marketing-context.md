## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Office furniture is expensive, overwhelming to shop for, and hard to get delivered/set up — especially from big box stores or online-only retailers.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Big box stores (IKEA, Office Depot): Cheap quality, no setup help, assembly required
- Online-only (Amazon, Wayfair): Can't see/try before buying, expensive shipping, no local support
- Other local dealers: Higher prices, limited selection, less experience
**What it costs them:** Wasted time, overpaying, cheap furniture that breaks, no one to call when something goes wrong
**Emotional tension:** Stress of spending thousands on furniture sight-unseen, fear of getting stuck with junk

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Other Utah office furniture dealers — FoxBuilt beats them on price, selection (new + pre-owned), and 25+ years experience
**Secondary:** Big box stores (IKEA, Office Depot, Costco) — decent prices but terrible quality, no service, no setup
