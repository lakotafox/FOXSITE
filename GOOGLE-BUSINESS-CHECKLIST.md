# Google Business Profile Optimization Checklist

This is the single most important thing for ranking in "office furniture near me" searches. Work through each item.

---

## Basic Info (Verify These Are Correct)
- [ ] Business name: **FoxBuilt Office**
- [ ] Primary category: **Office furniture store**
- [ ] Secondary categories: Furniture store, Used furniture store, Office supply store
- [ ] Address: 420 W Industrial Dr, Pleasant Grove, UT 84062
- [ ] Phone: (801) 899-9406
- [ ] Website: https://foxbuiltstore.com
- [ ] Hours: Mon–Fri 10am–5pm (mark Sat/Sun as Closed)

## Description
- [ ] Write a keyword-rich description (750 chars max). Include:
  - "office furniture" + "Utah" + "Salt Lake" + "Provo" + "Pleasant Grove"
  - New and pre-owned
  - Delivery and setup
  - Since 1999
  - Types of furniture (desks, chairs, cubicles, conference tables)

**Example:**
> FoxBuilt Office has been serving Utah businesses since 1999. Located in Pleasant Grove, we carry new and pre-owned office furniture including executive desks, ergonomic chairs, cubicle workstations, conference tables, and storage solutions. We offer delivery and professional setup across Utah County, Salt Lake County, and the Wasatch Front. Visit our showroom or call for a free quote.

## Photos (Google rewards profiles with 100+ photos)
- [ ] Upload 20-30+ high-quality showroom photos
- [ ] Add photos of: storefront/exterior, interior showroom, individual products, delivery/setup in action, team/staff
- [ ] Add a logo and cover photo
- [ ] Keep adding new photos monthly (Google ranks active profiles higher)

## Reviews (Most Important Ranking Factor)
- [ ] Respond to every existing review (even "Thanks, Kyle!" counts)
- [ ] Create a short review link to share with customers: go to GBP > "Ask for reviews" > copy link
- [ ] After every sale/delivery, text or email the review link to the customer
- [ ] Goal: 5+ new reviews per month
- [ ] Never offer incentives for reviews (violates Google policy)

## Posts (Free Advertising)
- [ ] Post at least once per week using the GBP "Updates" feature
- [ ] Post types: What's New, Offers, Events
- [ ] Include a photo + CTA button ("Call now" or "Learn more") in every post
- [ ] Reuse content from the SOCIAL-POSTS.md file

## Products
- [ ] Add 10-20 products directly in GBP (desks, chairs, tables, etc.)
- [ ] Include price ranges where possible (Google shows these in search)
- [ ] Link each product to the relevant page on foxbuiltstore.com

## Q&A Section
- [ ] Pre-seed the Q&A with the same questions from the website FAQ
- [ ] Answer your own questions (this is allowed and encouraged by Google)
- [ ] Monitor for new questions and respond quickly

## Services
- [ ] Add services: Office furniture sales, Delivery, Installation/Setup, Space planning, Bulk orders
- [ ] Add descriptions to each service

## Attributes
- [ ] Check all relevant attributes: Wheelchair accessible, Free parking, Veteran-owned (if applicable), etc.
- [ ] Add "Identifies as women-owned" or "Identifies as veteran-owned" if applicable

## Ongoing Maintenance
- [ ] Check GBP messages weekly (people message through Google Maps)
- [ ] Update hours for holidays
- [ ] Add new photos after every delivery/install
- [ ] Post weekly using social content
- [ ] Respond to all new reviews within 24 hours
- [ ] Check for and reject any suggested edits from Google/users that are wrong
